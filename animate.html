Drill Draw Animate — quick start

A whiteboard that moves. Drop pieces on the ice, draw where they travel, press play.

The layout

  1. Tool rail (far left) picks what you are doing. The panel next to it changes to match.
  2. Properties on the right edits whatever is selected; Surface sets the rink; Notes holds coaching points and equipment.
  3. The timeline along the bottom shows one bar per moving piece.

Marking up video

  1. File ▸ Open video… and pick an MP4 or WebM off your machine. Nothing uploads — the clip stays on your computer.
  2. The clip becomes the surface, and the timeline becomes the clip's length. Drag the playhead to scrub to the moment you want.
  3. Draw on it with any tool — arrows, circles, pen, labels. Marks sit still on a paused frame.
  4. To make a marker follow a player, drop a piece on them, then use Move to draw the route they take. Its bar on the timeline is in clip seconds, so you can line it up with exactly when the play happens.
  5. File ▸ Remove video puts you back on the rink.

The clip is not saved inside the drill file — a saved drill stores your marks, not the footage. Re-open the video and it lines back up.

Make a piece move

  1. Click Players in the rail, pick a skater, then click the ice to place it.
  2. Pick the Move tool and drag from that piece to draw where it travels.
  3. Press Play. The piece glides along the lane.
  4. A green bar appears on the timeline. Drag the bar to change when it starts; drag its right edge to change how long it takes. Or hit ⇥ Stagger to space everything out automatically.

The Skate, Pass, Shot, Arrow and Pen tools draw diagram lines only — they never move anything. Use Move for animation.

Carry & pass a puck

  1. Give your players their Move routes first.
  2. Select a puck. In Properties, Set carrier and click the player who starts with it.
  3. Add pass and click the receiver. Each pass shows as a diamond keyframe on the puck's timeline track — drag it to retime the pass.

Keep your work

Save downloads the drill as a file; Open loads it back. Space play, Esc deselect, Ctrl/⌘ Z undo.
